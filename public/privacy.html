Project SuperInk

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The short version

  • Project SuperInk is a free browser game made by Max Sabato. There are no ads, no tracking and nothing to buy with real money.
  • If you make a Splashburg ID, we store your username, email, password (handled securely by PlayFab) and your game progress.
  • If you play as a guest, we store a random ID and your game progress, with no email.
  • Some things are saved only in your own browser.
  • When you play online with friends, your browsers connect to each other, so they can see your internet (IP) address.
  • You must be 13 or older to play.
  • To ask a question or delete your account, open an issue on our GitHub Issues page.

Who we are

Project SuperInk ("the game", "we", "us") is a fan-made, non-commercial game created and run by Max Sabato in the United States. It is hosted at breeezyy111.github.io/Project-Superink. It is not affiliated with Nintendo.

We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, voice or payment details. The game has no chat. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

What is saved in your browser

The game uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember your profile and settings, whether you're signed in, and a few small helpers. This data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for the game. If you're playing without an account, that also deletes your progress.

Services we use

  • PlayFab (by Microsoft) runs Splashburg IDs, guest accounts, sign-in, password reset emails and cloud saves. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game's website. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to run and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • PeerJS helps browsers find each other for friends rooms. Its free public server, and its relay for networks that block direct connections, see your IP address while you're in a room. See peerjs.com.

These services handle data under their own privacy policies. Some of them may store data outside your country, including in the United States.

Playing online with friends

Friends rooms connect players' browsers directly to each other. That means the other players in your room, and the PeerJS servers that help connect you, can see your IP address. Only share room codes with people you know.

Who can see what

  • Your display name can be seen by other players in online rooms. Display names are unique, so someone could find out a name is taken.
  • Your email address and password are never shown to other players.
  • Max Sabato can see account information stored in PlayFab (such as your username, email, display name and game progress) to run and support the game.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress for as long as your account exists, so you can keep playing. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it from PlayFab. Data in your browser stays until you clear it. Server logs kept by PlayFab, GitHub and PeerJS are kept according to their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without an account: choose "Play as Guest". No email needed.
  • Sign out any time from your profile in the plaza.
  • See, correct or delete your data: open an issue on our GitHub Issues page. Include your in-game name, but never post your email address or password there, because issues are public. To make sure the account is yours, we may ask you to change your in-game name to a word we give you.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have extra rights, like the right to know what we hold about you or to have it deleted. We'll honor those requests sent through the same page.

Children

Project SuperInk is for players 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, tell us through the GitHub Issues page (without posting their personal details) and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are handled by PlayFab and are never stored by the game or visible to us. Connections to PlayFab use encryption (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll mention it in the game. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/breeezyy111/Project-Superink/issues.
